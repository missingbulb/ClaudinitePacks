# product-wiki — the self-growing product research wiki

- **Working on a requirement or spec** — read `product-wiki/product-requirements/` and the
  wikis it distills first; where the request contradicts or misses what the research found, say
  so to whoever raised it — the correction or the blind spot — rather than build past it.

- **Building on `product-wiki/` elsewhere** — only on
  `product-wiki/product-requirements/`, the human-reviewed sink; the wikis are unreviewed
  research and nothing may depend on them, which `product-wiki-isolation` enforces.
